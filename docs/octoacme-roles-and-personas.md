# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing leads ensure product quality and validate acceptance criteria. They design test strategies, coordinate testing activities, and report quality metrics. They work closely with developers and product managers to prevent defects from reaching production.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain test plans and test cases
- Execute unit, integration, and end-to-end testing
- Report bugs and quality metrics
- Validate acceptance criteria before PR approval
- Participate in release verification and smoke testing
- Coordinate with developers on testability and test automation

### Goals
- Catch defects early and reduce production issues
- Provide clear, actionable quality feedback to development teams
- Support timely, confident releases
- Maintain test coverage and quality standards

### Typical Communication
- Test status in daily standups
- Quality reports in weekly syncs
- Bug reports and defect tracking in issue management
- Collaboration with developers on test design and acceptance criteria

---

## Security/Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security/Compliance officers ensure projects meet security and regulatory standards. They review security risks, scan code, and validate compliance. They serve as the voice for security and regulatory requirements throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review security requirements and architecture
- Perform or coordinate security scanning in CI/CD
- Assess and mitigate security risks
- Support incident response for security-related issues
- Ensure compliance with regulatory and internal standards
- Advise on secure coding practices and dependency vulnerabilities

### Goals
- Prevent security vulnerabilities from reaching production
- Maintain customer trust and regulatory compliance
- Reduce security incident risk and business impact
- Foster a security-conscious development culture

### Typical Communication
- Security risk reviews in planning and kick-off
- Scanning results and remediation status in weekly syncs
- Incident escalation for security issues
- Security guidance and best practice recommendations to development team

---

## Technical/Architecture Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural guidance, ensure technical quality, and identify system-level risks. They mentor developers and oversee technical decision-making. They work with developers and project managers to balance quality, maintainability, and delivery timelines.

### Responsibilities
- Design and review system architecture
- Provide technical mentorship to developers
- Identify technical risks and propose solutions
- Oversee code quality standards and best practices
- Participate in design reviews and technical discussions
- Support capacity planning and technical feasibility assessments

### Goals
- Ensure scalable, maintainable system design
- Reduce technical debt and architectural risk
- Build team technical capability and ownership
- Enable sustainable delivery velocity

### Typical Communication
- Technical design docs and architecture reviews
- Code review feedback and mentorship
- Risk assessment in planning and weekly syncs
- Technical guidance and capability building with development team

---

## Sponsor/Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors and Key Stakeholders represent business priorities, provide approvals, and ensure alignment with organizational goals. They champion projects and ensure they deliver business value while maintaining organizational strategic alignment.

### Responsibilities
- Define business context and success criteria
- Provide approvals and prioritization guidance
- Communicate project status to broader organization
- Escalate blockers and resource constraints
- Champion project outcomes and adoption
- Support trade-off decisions and scope management

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value
- Maintain alignment with strategic priorities
- Enable cross-organization support and adoption
- Facilitate executive visibility and decision-making

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and status reviews
- Decision-making and approval gates
- Escalation for business-impacting issues
- Strategic alignment discussions and prioritization meetings

---

## Support/Customer Success Representative

### Role Summary
Support and Customer Success leads represent customer voice in projects and ensure solutions are operationally supportable. They coordinate go-to-market activities, gather customer feedback, and ensure the team considers customer needs and support implications throughout development.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and communicate customer needs and feedback
- Review feature design for support implications
- Coordinate release communications and training
- Prepare support documentation and runbooks
- Identify operability risks and support gaps
- Advocate for customer success and usability in product decisions

### Goals
- Ensure solutions meet real customer needs
- Reduce support burden and improve customer success
- Enable smooth customer adoption
- Build customer trust and satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback in planning and backlog prioritization
- Support readiness review before release
- Go-to-market coordination and customer communications
- Training and documentation collaboration with development team

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.

- Interactions between personas highlight cross-functional collaboration and communication patterns essential to project success.
